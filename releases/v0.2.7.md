# FoneClaw v0.2.7

## What's New

- Set up online AI models more easily with provider presets, and choose whether each model supports images and tools.
- Large photos selected for upload are resized and optimized when needed, with their orientation corrected.
- Browse the sidebar as one scrollable list while keeping key actions easy to reach at the bottom.
- Read update notes in your preferred language when a translation is available.
