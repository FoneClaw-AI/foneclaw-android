# v0.1.9 Release Notes

**Status:** Release candidate  
**Release date:** September 1, 2026  
**Compared with:** v0.1.8  
**Version code:** 20  
**Default APK:** Online TTS release

## Highlights

- Added coordinated domain agents that can handle specialized parts of complex requests while keeping progress and conversations visible.
- Improved the floating assistant with clearer worker conversations, voice composition, long-response viewing, copying, retrying, and feedback actions.
- Updated the account and subscription experience with clearer plan benefits, remaining points, reset time, and upgrade options.
- Improved on-screen navigation with directional scrolling and fresh-screen verification guidance.
- Improved AI-response feedback by including relevant conversation context in issue reports.
- Improved supporting services for subscription-plan display and AI response handling.
