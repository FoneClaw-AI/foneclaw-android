# FoneClaw 0.3.6

## What's new

- Share a news report or an individual story as text. When public sharing is enabled, eligible structured news can also become a readable public link, with each story's sources and publication time where valid. Personal notes and bookmark annotations are not included in the publication payload.
- Read older news reports more reliably, including supported historical tables and numbered formats. Unclear content stays readable as original text instead of being silently discarded or assigned invented fields.
- Fetching news again takes previous successful reports for that same occurrence into account and asks for distinct events rather than reworded repeats. Results still depend on available evidence and the model, not a guarantee of a full or duplicate-free report.
- See clearer waiting feedback while a managed model prepares its first reply, with improved handling of interrupted or invalid streamed responses.
- Get a confirmation dialog when a feature needs a subscription or more points, rather than being sent straight to purchase. Attachment drafts are retained when uploads fail on those entitlement checks, so you can retry explicitly.
- Save and edit travel plans more reliably, with clearer validation of itinerary activities, preparation and packing lists. Changes preserve unrequested saved content.
- Task-result notifications are grouped, and opening the group resolves to a currently posted result. Dismissing older notifications avoids acknowledging a newer result in their place.
- Eligible Free allowances can be granted again after the current Free entitlement expires when the service enables renewal. An active Free entitlement is not topped up just because its points run out. This is not unlimited usage or a promise of a fixed amount or renewal schedule.

## Availability and compatibility

- Public news links require a matching backend, publication storage, moderation and runtime configuration. Publication creation and the worker default to off, with runtime settings able to enable creation and worker processing. Guest creation is separately controlled. Text sharing does not require publishing a public page.
- Public links accept eligible structured news only, not every recovered historical report. Invalid or oversized content can be rejected, unsafe source URLs are not accepted, and ambiguous dates are not given an invented timezone. The public page's Listen, Report and Subscribe actions provide app-download guidance, not browser playback, report submission or subscription creation.
- Model waiting feedback requires the matching managed streaming service and negotiated support. It does not speed up upstream generation or guarantee an uninterrupted reply. Responses-based model streaming and tool continuation depend on the configured provider and compatible service, not a new universally available model.
- Free renewal depends on backend deployment, the enabled Free-grant configuration, account binding and verified eligibility. Paid/model access checks and finite allowances still apply. No permanent free voice service or unrestricted model access is promised.
- Online models continue to require HTTPS, including local-network and localhost endpoints. This requirement is inherited from 0.3.5, not introduced again in 0.3.6.
- Backend database changes require their matching migration. Downgrade compatibility and recovery after process termination are not guaranteed. Device behavior, upstream-provider compatibility and production deployment are separate from source-level checks.

## Distribution

- FoneClaw website builds use `ai.android.claw` with online and offline speech-synthesis variants. Offline synthesis does not make models, news or server-backed features work offline.
- Google uses `ai.foneclaw.lite` and Google Play billing/update paths. Shared news, subscription prompts, attachments, travel and notification changes apply, but external Android plugin execution and direct-only floating controls are not Play features. A Google AAB is a submission candidate, not proof of store rollout.
- CN uses `cn.android.claw`, domestic services and an independent updater, with no Firebase. Its shared direct-distribution features remain subject to permissions and network availability. The CN APK is not a default GitHub Release asset.
- App distribution, Google Play rollout and backend deployment are independent. Lebest remains discontinued.
