# FoneClaw v0.3.4

## What's new

- **Plan and explore trips**: Use links or selected photos to prepare a trip, save planning drafts, organize multi-city itineraries, and explore destination weather and attraction photos. Edit stops and preparation lists, or ask follow-up questions with the trip context.
- **More comfortable voice memos**: Improved text alignment, transcript and summary editing, elapsed-time display and scrolling. Linked to-dos open at their actual location.
- **Speech playback at a glance**: Media-style notifications show spoken replies and let you stop playback.
- **More reliable sign-in navigation**: Existing conversations, login and purchase pages better respect the current signed-in state.
- **Conversation refinements**: Improved tool-result presentation, status updates and news research response handling.

## Downloads and channel scope

- FoneClaw Online APK: online text-to-speech.
- FoneClaw Offline APK: offline text-to-speech.
- Google APK: the Google distribution for inspection or direct installation. The AAB is a separate Play submission candidate, not a GitHub asset. Google Play review and availability are independent of this release.
- CN uses the separate `cn.android.claw` package and domestic distribution infrastructure. Its APK is not included in these GitHub assets.

Travel research and image understanding depend on the enabled plugin, selected model, network and service availability. Shared recording UI and services are included in the Google build, subject to permissions and service configuration. Media notifications provide stop, not pause/resume, and do not guarantee uninterrupted lock-screen playback.

Backend deployment is separate. Search quota configuration requires the matching service release. Static publication and its worker default to disabled, while travel-community documents describe future work rather than an available community feature. Domestic service, payment and plugin-distribution acceptance remain separate channel gates.
