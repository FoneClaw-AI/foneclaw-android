# FoneClaw 0.3.8

## What’s new

- Enjoy more stable pet visuals: the companion avatar no longer flickers when you navigate back, and the animation loops blend more smoothly.
- When you switch pet companions in the startup guide (direct-distribution builds), the reply style automatically switches to that companion’s default, keeping look and style consistent.
- Reply style names now read more naturally across all languages (for example, “Playful and cute”).
- Starting a voice recording on the home screen automatically pauses any playing speech and resumes it when the recording finishes, so playback and recording no longer talk over each other.
- Convert news notes into to-dos with more accurate date recognition from the note’s leading date, plus smoother to-do updates and page jumps. Ambiguous prose dates still go through the existing confirmation flow.
- The direct-distribution download package is now about 40 MB while keeping the pet animations fully available offline.

## Availability and limits

- Companion appearance and reply style do not change tool permissions or approval rules. Bundled animation is not offline AI, automatic pet progression or unlimited memory.
- Playback pausing applies only while speech is actively playing and supports pausing; it does not change microphone permission requirements.
- Search, voice and model-backed work require network access, account eligibility, permissions and enabled services. Cloud/local web-reading fallback is not offline search and does not bypass authentication. Online-model endpoints still require HTTPS.
- Backend travel capabilities added this cycle (domestic scenic search and details, web-evidence fallback with cited reference cards, weather and ticket reference offers, licensed photos) are in development-environment acceptance and provider-configuration stage. They do not establish Android integration or production availability: current in-app travel research still uses web research. Development acceptance does not prove production rollout. Flights, trains, ticket offers and route matrices are not presented as complete bookable services.

## Channel scope

- FoneClaw direct builds include online and offline speech variants. Offline synthesis does not make online features offline. Direct-only floating controls and external Android APK plugins remain subject to permission and runtime gates. The startup-guide companion linkage applies to the refreshed guide used in direct builds.
- Google keeps its legacy startup guide, with companion selection in Assistant settings, and does not include the package-size change or the guide linkage fix. It includes the shared companion, news, task, Message Center and recording flows, not direct-only floating controls or external Android APK plugin execution. Google Play submission, review and availability are separate from APK distribution.
- CN uses independent domestic services and updates with no Firebase or FCM token provider. It retains shared built-in workflows and web-research fallback. Plugin repository access remains network-dependent.
- Backend source delivery is not production deployment. No seamless process-restart recovery, device-acceptance result or unconditional service availability is asserted. Lebest remains discontinued.
