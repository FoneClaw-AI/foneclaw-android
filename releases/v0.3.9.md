# FoneClaw 0.3.9

## What's new

- Plan domestic trips with richer reference details: regular opening hours, confirmed closures, last admission and official booking guidance for attractions, walking and driving estimate comparisons between nearby stops, and train or flight reference links (reference only, no live availability or prices).
- Ask free-form questions about visiting an attraction, such as opening hours and booking, and get a web-search summary with cited sources; details that can not be verified are clearly marked.
- The weather view now shows forecast status and data time, including available, partial, past date, too early and no-data states, and flags when an older forecast is being shown.
- Do more with chat messages: copy, select text, follow up with a quote and remove it, like or dislike replies, and edit a message into the composer to continue.
- Follow execution progress step by step: thinking, planning and tool steps show in-progress, succeeded, cancelled or failed states, and the stop button works per conversation so each assistant panel can stop its own running turn.
- Custom news, artist updates and competition updates share one task workspace and execution chain: failure cards show the real error of that run, interrupted runs prompt you to run again, missed catch-up windows show as skipped, and failed or skipped runs can be fetched again in place.
- Scheduled news omits items whose publication time can not be confirmed in the requested range and says so, marks stories whose date comes only from search results, lets you tap a run time to adjust the schedule and repeat rules, and may append one relevant feature link card to a reply that only opens the page.

## Availability and limits

- Travel visiting, weather and route references come from structured sources and verified web summaries served by the companion backend, which must be deployed and configured with its providers. Unavailable data is reported honestly. Train and flight links are references only, route estimates are not navigation promises, and photo display follows licensing.
- Message copy, quoted follow-ups and likes change local conversation presentation and the next request only; they never change tool permissions or approval rules.
- Stopping acts on the current conversation's run; final state is what actually happened, and background cleanup is not claimed to be immediate.
- News time filtering relies on confirmable publication times; unverified source dates are labeled as such. No fixed speed or completeness is promised.
- Online research, models, voice and sharing require network access, account eligibility, permissions and enabled services. Article sources may refuse access, cloud/local web-reading fallback is not offline search, and online-model endpoints still require HTTPS.

## Channel scope

- FoneClaw direct builds include online and offline speech variants and the domestic trip reference details above; offline speech synthesis does not make online features offline. Direct-only floating controls and external Android APK plugins remain subject to permission and runtime gates.
- Google builds keep the legacy startup guide overlay and the web-research travel path; the dedicated backend travel interfaces stay unavailable on that channel. Google Play submission, review and availability are separate from APK distribution.
- CN uses independent domestic services and updates with no Firebase or FCM token provider. It retains shared built-in workflows and web-research fallback. Plugin repository access remains network-dependent.
- Backend source delivery is not production deployment. No seamless process-restart recovery, device-acceptance result or unconditional service availability is asserted. Lebest remains discontinued.
