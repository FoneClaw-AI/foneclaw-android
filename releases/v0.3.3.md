# FoneClaw Android v0.3.3

FoneClaw 0.3.3 makes the assistant more personal, improves voice memos and task status clarity, and hardens conversation and notification stability.

- Open the new Assistant page from the sidebar to choose an avatar and name, shape the personality, adjust voice output, pick a model, and manage the floating assistant.
- Manage what the assistant remembers — basic info, daily habits and communication style — with simple add, edit, delete and on/off controls.
- Read voice memo details more easily: unified spacing and link styles, crossed-out completed action items, and cleaner editing. Re-summarizing refreshes action items while keeping your real to-dos.
- Add and complete to-dos without layout flicker; a failed navigation save keeps your draft so you can retry.
- See a clear "finished · not completed" task state, and get reminded of unfinished tasks before a new run starts.
- Play a voice reply manually regardless of the auto-play setting.
- Switch between models with different tool capabilities and keep the conversation compatible automatically; notifications are more reliable and managed speech playback keeps its moderation authorization.

This release does not claim completed production backend deployment for service-dependent behavior. Google Play publication and review are separate channel gates.
