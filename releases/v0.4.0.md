# FoneClaw 0.4.0

## What's new

- Manage to-dos by day or list, mark them complete or active, and add or edit them manually or with the assistant.
- Review and edit personal memories. Smart memory reviews conversations and handles similar suggestions more carefully when the relevant mode, model, and services are available; it does not remember every conversation automatically.
- Get clearer scheduled-news results: improved source and publication-time checks, and an explanation when fewer stories than requested can be supported. An additional search may be attempted when an explicitly requested count falls short; the count is not guaranteed.
- In direct-distribution builds, camera capture may fall back to in-app capture when an explicitly requested front or back lens cannot be confirmed in the system camera. Photos saved by the app remain viewable with limited gallery access, subject to device and permission limits.

## Availability and compatibility

- The camera/gallery changes apply to FoneClaw direct and CN builds, not the Google build. Google shares the to-do, memory and scheduled-news improvements but not direct-only floating controls or external Android APK plugins. CN uses independent services and updates without Firebase. Lebest remains discontinued.
- News needs accessible sources; smart memory requires the corresponding setting and compatible model/service. Online-model endpoints still require HTTPS. Offline speech does not make online features available offline.
- The companion backend includes configurable model-level speech/voice routing, subject to deployment, database migration, administrator configuration and upstream availability. Source delivery does not establish production deployment or an enabled user-facing voice feature.
- Memory storage moves to Room schema v3 with a forward migration; rollback compatibility has not been established. Backend database migrations V56–V63 require separate deployment verification. Google Play submission, review and availability are separate from the candidate app build.
