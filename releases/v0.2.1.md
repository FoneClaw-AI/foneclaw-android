# v0.2.1 Release Notes

**Status:** Release candidate  
**Release date:** September 11, 2026  
**Compared with:** v0.2.0  
**Version code:** 22  
**Default APK:** Online TTS release

## Highlights

- Redesigned To-do Center with an Unscheduled section, clearer date-based organization, faster completion, and easier editing.
- Improved email summaries with accurate Inbox/Sent counts, useful previews, and fewer duplicate reads or approval prompts.
- Improved points and subscription guidance with clearer balances, plan choices, purchase recovery, and post-task refresh.
- Improved long-running cloud tasks with clearer waiting, cancellation, recovery, and completion handling.
- Improved account entitlement synchronization and compatibility for existing guest and member accounts.
